ThumbiT Executive is an executive saloon, a luxury car or an executive people carrier from a licensed local firm. You choose the car and see its fixed price before you book.

Three kinds of car

Executive saloon

A Mercedes E-Class, BMW 5 Series or similar.

For the airport, a meeting, or a journey you'd like to be quiet and comfortable.

Luxury car or limousine

A Mercedes S-Class, a limousine, or a wedding and event car.

For the days that matter: weddings, proms, anniversaries, and guests you want to look after.

Executive people carrier

A Mercedes V-Class or similar.

For a group travelling together, with room for everyone's luggage.

Each firm lists the cars it runs, so the car you see when you book is the kind of car that arrives. Executive cars are offered where a local firm provides them.

How you book one

  1. Enter your journey

    Pickup, destination and when, in the ThumbiT app.

  2. Choose your car

    On the price screen you see each local firm's executive cars, with a fixed price for your journey.

  3. Travel

    Your booking goes to the firm you chose. Follow your driver on the map as they arrive.

What you can count on

A fixed price

The firm's own price for your journey, shown before you book. Your booking goes to that firm only, and that price is what is recorded when the journey ends.

Licensed firms and drivers

Every executive journey is private hire, with a licensed local firm and a licensed driver.

A standard taxi if no executive car is free

We ask you first, and show you the standard price. Nothing changes unless you say yes.

Electric or hybrid, if you prefer

On any booking you can say you'd prefer an electric or hybrid car. One is sent when it's free.

A £1.50 booking fee is paid in the app. Your fare is paid to the driver unless the app says otherwise.

For firms with executive cars

The Executive fleet is an add-on to ThumbiT dispatch. It puts your executive cars in front of local passengers, at prices you set yourself.

  • Mark each car as an executive saloon, a luxury car or limousine, or an executive people carrier.
  • Set your own price per mile and minimum fare for each kind of car.
  • Executive bookings are private hire at your agreed price, and are only offered to a driver in the right kind of car.
  • Your office can add executive bookings on the dispatch desk in the same way.
Ask about the Executive fleet

The Executive fleet is switched on for each firm by ThumbiT. Email support@thumbittaxis.com and we'll talk it through.
